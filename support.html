Before you contact us

  • Check the guide for adding, using and removing the app.
  • In the app, open Settings → Log and include the last lines of the log in your message. The log contains no passwords or tokens.
  • Tell us the app version (shown next to the app name) and whether the meeting belongs to your own Zoom account or another account.

Common questions

The bot cannot join the meeting. The Zoom user who is signed in to the app must already be in the meeting before the app joins. If that user leaves, the app has to leave as well.

The bot is in the meeting but there is no video. The meeting host must allow livestreaming or recording for the app (or make it co-host), depending on the access mode selected in the app.
