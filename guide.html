Guide: LUXAV Group Engine for Zoom

Adding, using and removing the app

LUXAV Group Engine is a Windows application for live event production. It joins a Zoom meeting on behalf of a signed-in Zoom user, composes the participants' video into one group layout and sends it to production software such as vMix via OMT or NDI.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with the LUXAV Group Engine installed (portable folder, no installation needed).
  • A Zoom account that will attend the meeting, for example the production desk's account.
  • For video access, the meeting host must allow livestreaming or local recording for participants.

Adding the app

  1. Start LUXAV Group Engine and open the Zoom tab.
  2. Click Sign in with Zoom ("Mit Zoom anmelden"). Your browser opens the Zoom authorization page.
  3. Sign in with your Zoom account and click Allow. The app requests permission to read your user profile (name and e-mail, shown in the app) and to request meeting tokens on your behalf.
  4. The browser returns to the app, which now shows the account you are signed in with. The sign-in is kept on this PC until you sign out.

Using the app

  1. Join the meeting with the Zoom account you signed in with (for example in the Zoom client on the same PC or on a phone). This account must be in the meeting before the app joins.
  2. In the app, enter the meeting ID or invitation link and the passcode, choose the access mode (Livestream or Recording) and click Join.
  3. As host, confirm the request Allow livestream or Allow recording for the app, or make it co-host. Participants see the usual Zoom notice that the meeting is being livestreamed or recorded.
  4. Participants with their camera on appear in the group layout automatically. Use the Live tab to pin, feature or hide participants, correct display names and adjust the layout.
  5. Click Leave to end. If the signed-in account leaves the meeting, the app leaves too.

Removing the app

  1. In the app, open the Zoom tab and click Sign out ("Abmelden"). This deletes the stored sign-in from the PC and revokes it at Zoom.
  2. To remove the app from your Zoom account completely, sign in to the Zoom App Marketplace, go to Manage → Added Apps, find LUXAV Group Engine and click Remove.
  3. Optionally delete the data folder of the app to remove local settings such as display names and rules.

Removing the app stops all access to your Zoom account. No data is kept by LUXAV, because the app does not send data to LUXAV servers. See the privacy policy.
