2. What the app does

The app joins a Zoom meeting on behalf of the signed-in Zoom user, receives the participants' video (and optionally the mixed meeting audio) through Zoom's raw data interface after the meeting host has granted permission, and composes it into a group video layout. The result is sent as a video signal (OMT or NDI) to production software, such as vMix, in the operator's local network.

3. Data processed and why

  • Zoom account data of the signed-in user (name, e-mail address) via the scope user:read:user, only to show in the app which account is signed in.
  • Zoom tokens via the scope user:read:token: an OAuth refresh token and short-lived access tokens, used to request an "on behalf of" (OBF) token for the specific meeting the app joins. The refresh token is stored only on the operator's PC, encrypted with Windows DPAPI and bound to the Windows user account.
  • Meeting data: participant display names, camera and speaking status, participant video and optionally mixed meeting audio. This is processed in memory on the operator's PC to produce the live output. The app itself does not record or store video or audio.
  • Local settings created by the operator, such as corrected display names, rules for hiding or pinning participants by name and layout settings. They are stored in local files on the operator's PC.

4. No servers, no tracking

The app does not send meeting content, account data or usage data to LUXAV or to any third party. There is no telemetry, analytics or advertising. Data is exchanged only with Zoom, as required to sign in and to join meetings, and with the production systems the operator configures in the local network.

After the Zoom sign-in, Zoom redirects the browser to a static forwarding page hosted on GitHub Pages, which passes the one-time authorization code to the app on the same PC. The page stores nothing. GitHub may process technical data such as the IP address when the page is loaded; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Signing out in the app deletes the stored token from the PC and revokes it at Zoom.
  • Removing the app in the Zoom App Marketplace (Manage → Added Apps → Remove) revokes its access to the Zoom account.
  • Local settings can be deleted at any time by deleting the app's data folder.
  • Video and audio are not retained by the app. If the output is recorded in downstream production software, that recording is the responsibility of the event organizer.

6. Meeting participants

The app joins only meetings in which the signed-in user is present, and it is visible in the participant list. Zoom shows participants that the meeting is being recorded or livestreamed while the app receives video. The event organizer is responsible for informing participants about the production and for the legal basis of using their image.
